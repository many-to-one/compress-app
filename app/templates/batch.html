Image Compression

Why reduce image size?

  • Faster websites — smaller images load instantly and improve SEO.
  • Less storage usage — save space on servers, clouds and devices.
  • Better performance — apps and galleries open faster.
  • Quicker sharing — upload and send images much faster.
  • Lower bandwidth — ideal for mobile users and slow networks.

FAQ — Image Compression & WebP

This tool compresses and optionally converts your images to modern formats like WebP to reduce file size while keeping visual quality.

Yes. You can compress images in their original format or convert them to WebP for even smaller file sizes.

Smaller images mean faster websites, better UX, improved SEO, lower bandwidth usage and smoother galleries or apps.

WebP is a modern image format that offers much better compression than JPG or PNG. Use it for web, apps and any place where performance matters.
